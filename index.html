The Story Behind Prabhat

Built by people who believe business is built on relationships.

The story of Prabhat began with effort, patience and a simple belief: sell what is right for the customer. From a workshop in Pipliya Mandi in 1990 to a small Prabhat Steel shop of less than 200 sq. ft., the business grew step by step through trust and hard work.

In 2006, Prabhat came to Mandsaur. In 2014, electronics and utensils were added with the vision of offering every important home need under one roof. Today, Prabhat Steel and Wooden Furniture works from more than 11,000 sq. ft. of constructed area with 5 departments across multiple floors.
